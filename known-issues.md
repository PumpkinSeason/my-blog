## Theme switch

The theme switch sometimes paints the wrong colours for a moment, or needs a
second tap.

Because the choice is saved in your browser (local storage) and the page has to
read it before it can paint. Until that happens it doesn't know whether you want
day, evening or night.

It's a trade-off: no cookies, no trackers, no server. Your preference stays on
your device — which also means the page can't know it in advance.

Hope it doesn't annoy you too much.
